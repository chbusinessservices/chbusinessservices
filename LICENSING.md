## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I modify the code under Proprietary license?
**A:** No. Proprietary licenses are read-only. For modifications, upgrade to Commercial or White-Label.

### Q: Does White-Label include customer support?
**A:** Email and phone support included. 24/7 SLAs available under Enterprise licensing.

### Q: Can I resell a White-Label license?
**A:** Not the license itself, but you can resell services/products built with the licensed software (subject to agreement terms).

### Q: What happens if I violate license terms?
**A:** Legal action may be taken, including injunctions and damages. Confidentiality agreements are binding.

### Q: Do licenses include updates?
**A:** Yes. Security patches included in all tiers. Feature updates depend on your tier (included in Commercial+).

### Q: Can I get a free evaluation?
**A:** Contact us for a limited-time evaluation agreement (2-4 weeks, review-only).
